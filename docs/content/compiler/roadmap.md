## Approach

The compiler is built to the [architecture](/compiler/) from the first stage: every program, from
`main() = 42` on, goes through the real pipeline — resolve, check, lower to MIR, emit — and is linked
against the real runtime. Early stages make that pipeline thin and end-to-end; later stages widen
it. No stage builds a temporary path that a later stage has to tear out.

## Stages

Each stage is one or more bounded items. Each ends green on the full gate and is merged to `dev`
with `--ff-only`.

| # | Stage | Delivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **Specification** | This specification, the architecture, this roadmap, the project reset |
| 1 | **Skeleton and test harness** | The `compiler/`, `runtime/` and `library/` packages; the lexer and parser carried over; `avin parse`, `check` and `build`; a thin end-to-end pipeline (`main() = 42` and `println` of a literal) through resolve, check, MIR and the LLVM emitter, linked with a minimal runtime; the Sysl-written execution-test driver in `sysl test` (`.avin` cases with expected output, run at `-O0` and `-O2`) |
| 2 | **Type core and scalar programs** | The `Type` enum over symbols; scopes and symbol tables; local bidirectional inference with subtype constraints and `Nothing`; integers with trapping overflow, `byte`, `real`, `char`, `bool`, `unit`; functions, local functions, `if`/`match`/loops, guards, scalar patterns and coverage; unbounded generic functions; by-name parameters. The old execution corpus, revised to the specification, joins the suite here |
| 3 | **Modules and packages** | `avin.hocon` loading, multi-file modules, imports and visibility, the prelude as an Avin file, `@intrinsic` declarations |
| 4 | **Runtime and heap values** | The collector over `sh.sysl.gc` with shadow-stack roots; type descriptors; traps with messages and locations; `string` as a runtime value, with `s"…"` rendering the built-in types; `data` layouts, tuples, lambdas and closures with environments; by-name parameters |
| 5 | **Object model** | `class`, `trait`, `object`, `impl`, methods, `override`, `open`/`abstract`, constructors with checked initialization, companions, member access, type patterns; overloading, default and named arguments; infix method calls |
| 6 | **Monomorphization and dispatch** | Bounded generics, closed-world instantiation, dispatch for generic trait methods, reified headers, variance checking, devirtualization |
| 7 | **Sysl bindings** | `@sysl` declarations, shim generation, the cached shim build, boundary conversions, opaque handles, diagnostics mapped back to bindings |
| 8 | **Core library in Avin** | `Eq`, `Hash`, `Ord`, `Display`, `Debug`, `Writer`, `Option`, `Result`, `Either`, `?`, string methods, `println`, `debug`, `StringContext` and interpolators, interpolation and formatting |
| 9 | **Collections in Avin** | `Iterable`/`Iterator` (Iterator is Iterable), `Seq`, `List`, `Vector`, `Array`, `Buffer`, `Deque`, `Set`, `Map`, `Range`, builders, views, literals built through them, `::`, list and sequence patterns, `for` comprehensions |
| 10 | **Test runner and tooling** | `avin test`, `avin run`, project and package commands, `avin lsp` |
| 11 | **System library** | `avin.io`, `avin.fs`, `avin.process`, `avin.time` and the rest over Sysl bindings |
| 12 | **Actors and state machines** | `actor` declarations, messages and their scheduling in the runtime; `machine` and `state` declarations, transitions and their checks |
| 13 | **C bindings** | `@c` bindings called directly from Avin's code, checked against the library's headers by a clang-compiled C unit; C libraries in the manifest; handles, strings, out parameters, buffers and callbacks from C threads; `avin-lang/mlt` (MLT 7) and `avin-lang/cairo` (Cairo 1.18) as the stressors, with `avin-lang/freetype` (FreeType 2), which Cairo's FreeType font faces depend on |
| 14 | **Lightweight threads** | Every thread a lightweight thread with a stack of its own, run by a scheduler over a few operating system threads, waits that park, time-slicing at safepoints, sockets on a poller, actors' turns and the server library on top, as [settled](/spec/decisions/) |

Until stage 8, `println` in stage 1 was an `@intrinsic` over a runtime entry point, declared in Avin
source like every other intrinsic; stage 8 replaced it with the library's `println` over `Display`.

### Dates and times: build plan

`avin.time` is rebuilt to [its chapter](/spec/time/) over `@sysl` bindings to Sysl 0.10.0's
`sysl.time` and `sysl.posix.time`, with no new C. Each step is one item, gated on its own, and
ships the tests below for what it builds; a binding that cannot hold Sysl's `Zone` as a handle is a
gap to report, not to route around.

1. **Clocks and durations.** `Instant(epochMicros)` as the wall clock, `WallTime` and the monotonic
   `Instant` removed; `now()`, `monotonic() -> Duration`, `epochSeconds`/`epochMillis`/`epochMicros`;
   the makers `micros millis seconds minutes hours days weeks` (from `microseconds` and
   `milliseconds`) and their methods on `int` and `long`; `toX()`/`toXPart()`; `d / e`; the
   duration's and instant's text; `Duration.Zero` in place of `zero()`. Every caller moves with it:
   `avin.process`'s command deadline (a `monotonic()` reading), `avin.actors`, `avin.net`'s
   `setTimeout`, the tests and examples that name `milliseconds`, `zero()`, `wallClock`, `elapsed`
   or `WallTime` (`git grep` them), and the `Duration` overloads of `await` and `waitFor`.
2. **The calendar.** `Date`, `Time`, `DateTime`, `Weekday`: makers and their traps, fields,
   `with…`, `startOf…`, `endOfMonth`, `onOrAfter`/`onOrBefore`, `isoWeek`, `daysUntil`,
   `DateTime ± Duration`, the ISO 8601 display.
3. **Zones.** `Zone` over the host's database, `utc`, `localZone()`, `fixedZone`, `ZoneError`,
   `Resolution`, `Zoned` with `atZone`/`atZoneEarlier`/`atZoneLater`, `ZoneTimeError`, its equality
   and order, `Zoned ± Duration`, abbreviation and daylight saving.
4. **Periods.** `Period`, `months`/`years`, `plus…`, `Date`/`DateTime`/`Zoned ± Period` with the
   month-end clamp and the gap and overlap rules, `until`/`monthsUntil`.
5. **Text in.** `parseDate`, `parseTime`, `parseDateTime`, `parseTimestamp`, `parseZoned`,
   `TimeParseError`; `format(pattern)`.

  `int | long` is the int, `5_000_000_000` the long, `3` into `long | string` the long, `3` into
  `byte | long` rejected, `1.5` into `real | string`, `int.MaxValue`/`long.MinValue` boundaries;
  `None`, `[]` and `Ok(1)` into a union with one fitting member, and rejected with two; `int | string`
  into `int | string | bool`; `int | bool` into `int | string` rejected.
- *Subtyping and variance*: `Dog | Cat` passed as `Animal`; `int | long` as `Any` and matched back;
  `int | string` as `Display`; `List[Dog] <: List[Dog | Cat]`, `Option[Dog | Cat] <: Option[Animal]`,
  `Result[T, IoError] <: Result[T, IoError | ParseError]` with no conversion (identity preserved:
  the same `Array` inside); `List[int]` as `List[int | string]` rejected, `List[int | string]` as
  `List[int | string | bool]` rejected; `Array[Dog]` as `Array[Dog | Cat]` rejected (invariant);
  `(Dog | Cat) -> int` as `(Dog) -> int`; `() -> Dog` as `() -> Dog | Cat`; `(int | string) -> int`
  value as `(int) -> int` rejected, the named function accepted; `@sendable` union functions.
- *Overloads*: `show(1)`/`show("a")`/`show(true)` pick int / union / Any; `f(int | string)` vs
  `f(int | bool)` with `1` ambiguous and with a union argument resolved; member overloads with a
  union argument rejected suggesting `match`; generic overload `g[T](x: T)` vs `g(x: int | string)`;
  overloads differing by member order rejected as duplicates.
- *match and exhaustiveness*: every member by type pattern; a member left out names `_: bool`; `_`
  covers the rest; a supertype pattern covers a member; a subtype pattern needs the rest; a
  sub-union pattern binds the smaller union and leaves the others; literal, range, comparison,
  `n + k`, constructor, list, sequence, tuple and extractor patterns each reach their one member and
  count only for it; `0` on `int | long` rejected; `Some(x)` on `Option[int] | Option[string]`
  rejected; a type pattern naming no member rejected; unreachable arm after its member is covered
  rejected; guards do not cover; a `Parrot` in `Named | Pet` taken by the first arm; clauses by member
  with a missing member rejected; `match` functions (`match n -> …`) over a union; an `Any` matched
  by `x: (int | string)` binds the union and fails on a `bool`; a generic `match` on `T | int` checked
  once and, at `T = int`, taking every value by the `T` arm.
- *is*: `if v is s: string`, `is not` with early return, `while v is n: int`, `&&` and `||` scopes;
  `v.charCount()` after the test rejected (no narrowing).
- *Equality and hashing*: same member equal and unequal; different members unequal (`int` 1 vs
  `long` 1 in `int | long`); `u == 3` injects; `u == true` rejected; a class member with its own `Eq`
  used; the sequence exception (`Vector[int] | List[int]`); `Set[int | string]` holding `1` and `"1"`;
  `Map[int | string, V]` lookups; hash equals the member's hash; `===` on `Dog | Cat` accepted and on
  `Dog | int` rejected.
- *Traits and member calls*: `println`, interpolation, `toString()`, `debug` (strings quoted) and a
  field width on unions; `[T: Display]` at a union; a union without `Display` member not displayable
  (reject naming the member); a library trait with receiver-only `Self` reached; `Ord` refused
  (`sorted()` on `Vector[int | string]` rejected, `TreeMap` key rejected); `Sendable` through
  `spawn`/an actor message when every member is, and rejected when one is not; `a.name()` through the
  join; `v.charCount()` rejected; a name on two conforming traits rejected.
- *Derivations*: a data type and a class with a union field derive `Eq`, `Hash`, `Display`, `Debug`;
  `derives Ord` rejected naming the field; a derive template's bound met and not met by a union
  field.
- *`?`*: rule 1 with two error kinds; a subclass error into its superclass member; a union `?`'d
  inside a wider union; rule 2 (`From` on one member) and two members with `From[E]` rejected; rule
  3 (union into a wrapper with two `From`s) and a member with no conversion rejected naming it; rule
  4 unchanged; `NetError` into `IoError | ParseError` rejected with the message; `?` in a lambda with
  a declared union result; a returned `Result[T, IoError]` widened with no conversion; a scalar error
  union rebuilt with `mapErr(e -> e)` and rejected without it; `Option` `?` unaffected.
- *Representation and GC*: a reference union and a scalar union in locals, fields, tuples, `Vector`,
  `List`, `Array`, `Buffer`, `Map` values, closures' captures and actor messages, held across forced
  collections (strings and class instances in the reference slot stay alive and intact); a scalar
  union of `long | real | char | bool | unit` round-tripping every member and boundary value
  (`long.MinValue`, `real` NaN and -0.0, `'\u{10FFFF}'`); a union nested in a union field of a data
  type nested in a vector; generic `Vector[T | int]` at `T = string` and at `T = int`; the same output
  at `-O0` and `-O2`.
- *with and aliases*: `p with { v = 3 }` injects; `p with { v.x = 1 }` through a union rejected;
  `type Num = int | long` in every position; generic alias `type OrErr[T] = T | string`; `impl` on
  an alias of a union rejected.
- *Closures*: lambdas taking and returning unions; a closure capturing a union `var` and changing it
  to another member seen outside; placeholder lambdas over a union (`xs.map(_.toString())`).
- *Refusals*: `impl Display for int | string`, an extension on a union, `class C: A | B`,
  `[T: A | B]` (and `[T >: A | B]` accepted), a union in an `@sysl` and an `@c` parameter, result and
  mirror field, calling or constructing a union, each with its message.
